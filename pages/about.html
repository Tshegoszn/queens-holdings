01 / Our story

A practical approach
to better business.

Queen's Holdings (Pty) Ltd is a South African-based consulting and supply company established in 2021 and headquartered in Hartbeespoort.

The company operates nationally, providing reliable and efficient services to government entities, state-owned enterprises and private-sector clients.

Queen's Holdings specialises in management consulting and the strategic sourcing and supply of goods, delivering integrated solutions that support operational efficiency and business continuity.
